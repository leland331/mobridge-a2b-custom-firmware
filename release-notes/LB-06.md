# LB-06 / 2.11.14

Bug-fix firmware based on stock **2.11.8**.

**Supported device:** DA-G2.Pro A2B Ford, product ID `0x202c` only.

## Fixes

- Parked wake fault that could leave the amps off and audio silent.
- Amp remote cycling at ignition-on.
- Shutdown snaps caused by power and DSP shutdown timing.
- A light ignition-on snap when the amps were already on.

It does not add new audio features.

## Testing

Installed and tested by the maintainer on one setup, with continued good operation reported. It has not been broadly validated across vehicles or hardware revisions.
