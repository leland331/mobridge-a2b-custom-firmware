# LB-RC1 / 2.12.0 (Beta)

Feature firmware based on **LB-06 / 2.11.14** (all its fixes included).

**Supported device:** DA-G2.Pro A2B Ford, product ID `0x202c` only.

## New

- **Rear Chimes:** park-assist and cross-traffic tones can play on the rear outputs you route them to, with their
  own level. Until routed, rear tones are silent; all other chimes behave as before.
- **Live meters for outputs 9-12**, alongside 1-8.

Routing Rear Chimes needs an app that supports it. The official app may clear the Rear Chimes routing when it
saves routing.
